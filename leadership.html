PedaForge Leadership

AI Coaching & Curriculum Leadership Sandbox - giving Centre Directors the continuous observation data and SFw-aligned tools to coach educators, not just appraise them.

Overview AI Coach LNA & IDP AI Lesson Observation Dashboard
The Challenge

The Emotional Exhaustion Crisis

Across Busy Bees' centres, curriculum leadership is the difference between a teacher who stays and one who burns out. But our Directors do not have the bandwidth - or the data - to coach every educator, every day.

Subjective

Appraisals Without Evidence

A 2025 ECDA × SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG) joint report found leaders struggle to translate subjective annual appraisals into targeted Continuing Professional Development (CPD), because they lack continuous observation data on what is actually happening in the classroom.

No Bandwidth

Retention Hinges on Mentorship

Today, in February 2026, teacher retention hinges on supportive curriculum leadership and non-punitive mentorship. Yet Centre Directors lack the bandwidth for the daily micro-coaching that keeps educators growing, supported, and in the profession.

The Solution

Three Modules That Turn Observation Into Growth

PedaForge Leadership equips every educator with a QTT-aligned coaching agent, gives Directors a full-cycle AI lesson-observation workflow, and closes the loop with a three-step curriculum-leadership engine mapped to the Skills Framework for ECCE.

1

QTT-Aligned Coaching Agent & Accounts

Every educator receives an individualised, secure account. An AI chatbot cross-references the teacher's own lesson against the ECDA Quality Teaching Tool (QTT) and offers real-time, actionable suggestions to strengthen the Key Person role and specific QTT indicators - supporting, never replacing, the educator's professional agency.

QTT AI Coach Secure Accounts
Open the AI Coach
2

Full-Cycle AI Lesson Observation Analysis

A complete observation workflow that integrates each teacher's Learning Needs Analysis (LNA) and Individual Development Plan (IDP). Three connected stages take a Director from preparation to evidence to a ready-to-share development plan.

1
Before

Pre-Observation

Surfaces the educator's existing IDP goals and recommends the QTT domains most worth focusing on for this visit.

2
During

Actual Observation

The Director captures multimodal voice and text notes on a high-performance device; the AI tags evidence to QTT indicators in real time.

3
After

Post-Observation

The AI drafts a feedback summary and a follow-up development plan, ready for the Director to refine and share.

See the Observation Flow
3

Director's 3-Step Curriculum Leadership

A leadership engine that turns individual observations into centre-wide capability building, in three deliberate steps.

  1. A

    Strengths & Weaknesses Profiling

    A live Pedagogical Profile per staff member, built from coaching and observation evidence.

  2. B

    Pedagogical Collaboration Topics

    Profiles aggregate to reveal centre-wide gaps - for example, Formative Assessment - surfaced as a ready-made PLC topic or peer-mentor pairing.

  3. C

    Targeted SFw Professional Development

    QTT weaknesses are mapped to the Skills Framework for ECCE, recommending specific WSQ courses - e.g. "Engage Children in Meaningful Conversations" (TSC ECE-PIN-3001-1.1).

SFw for ECCE WSQ

Architecture & Compliance

Enterprise-Grade, Singapore-Resident, PDPA-Compliant

Leadership runs on the same React + FastAPI stack and Azure AI Enclave as PedaForge Classroom, with leadership-specific safeguards for sensitive appraisal data.

React + FastAPI + Azure AI Enclave

A React front end and FastAPI back end on a closed-tenant Azure AI Enclave host coaching, observation, and SFw mapping in one secure environment.

React FastAPI Azure AI

PDPA & SG Data Residency

All educator, observation, and appraisal data is hosted in Singapore-based data centres and handled in strict accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

SG Region PDPA

RBAC & Director Oversight

Role-based access control gives Directors auditable oversight while keeping sensitive appraisal data isolated, so each user sees only their authorised cohorts.

RBAC Audit Trail

Zero Public AI Training

PedaForge uses Azure OpenAI within a closed tenant - no educator, child, or appraisal data is ever used to train public models.

Closed Tenant No Public Training
Hardware Provision

Equipping the Leaders, Not Just the System

High-Performance Laptops

One high-performance laptop is provided to each Centre Director. These devices power live multimodal observation capture - voice and text notes recorded at the point of teaching - and run the on-device and cloud AI processing that tags evidence to QTT indicators in real time.

Centre Directors Multimodal Capture

Why Director-Grade Devices

  • ✓Real-time AI tagging during observation demands responsive, high-spec hardware.
  • ✓Multimodal voice-and-text capture works reliably across noisy early childhood environments.
  • ✓Secure, RBAC-protected access to isolated appraisal data on a managed device.
  • ✓One device per Director - no shared-cost double-counting with other proposals.
