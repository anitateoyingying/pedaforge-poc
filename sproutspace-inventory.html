Active Resource Inventory

Every learning resource in the centre carries a QR tag. Educators check items in and out from any device, log condition and location, and draw on a shared repository of teacher-made resources - so the right materials reach the right children, and nothing is bought twice.

What the Inventory Portal Delivers

Every check-in, check-out, and condition rating is captured by the QR portal, giving leadership a live, evidence-based view of how resources move and wear across the centre - without a single manual stock-take.
