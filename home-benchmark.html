For educators

Star Check is the termly benchmark, done with the child. Sprout = Emerging, Leaf = Developing, Flower = Secure across the five early-reading strands. Only your taps are saved to the child's record - the three little games can suggest a band for the Sound Star (Phonics), but you decide whether to accept it. Formative and supportive, never a high-stakes test.
