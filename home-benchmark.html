Collaborative Reading Strategies

Simple, evidence-based moves parents can try tonight — pick the ones that match the child’s developing strands.

1 Echo Reading

Read a short line aloud first, then have the child echo it straight back — matching your pace and expression. Builds fluent decoding without the pressure of going it alone.

2 Pause & Predict

Stop at an exciting moment and ask “What do you think happens next?” Accept any reasonable guess. This directly grows the comprehension strand.

3 Point & Read

Run a finger under each word as you read together. Slows reading to one word at a time and reinforces sight words in context.
