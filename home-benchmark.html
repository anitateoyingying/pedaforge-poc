Suggested summary for parents (plain language)
Aisyah is reading right on track for K2 and clearly loves books. She handles a book like a confident reader - finding the title, tracking the words and reading happily at home and in class. Her letter sounds are strong and secure. The next step is blending those sounds smoothly into whole words and building up her bank of sight words like “said” and “they”. In stories she answers “who” and “where” well; gently inviting her to predict what happens next will stretch her comprehension. A few minutes of shared reading each day, with a pause to guess what comes next, will make a real difference this term.
This summary is advisory only. The AI drafts language from the teacher’s and parents’ own evidence; the educator reviews, edits and confirms it before anything is shared. Benchmarking here is formative - a supportive snapshot to guide reading together, never a high-stakes test or a ranking of children.

Collaborative Reading Strategies

Simple, evidence-based moves parents can try tonight - chosen to lift Aisyah’s next strands.

1 Echo Reading

Read a short line aloud first, then have Aisyah echo it straight back - matching your pace and expression. Builds fluent decoding without the pressure of going it alone.

2 Pause & Predict

Stop at an exciting moment and ask “What do you think happens next?” Accept any reasonable guess. This directly grows the comprehension strand she is still emerging in.

3 Point & Read

Run a finger under each word as you read together. Slows reading to one word at a time and reinforces sight words like “said” and “they” in context.
