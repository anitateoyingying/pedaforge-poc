For educators

Paint Corner is the Draw, Write and Reflect SEL module. Each saved picture carries the feeling the child chose (Happy, Okay or Tricky), the story they told out loud if they used the microphone, and a short AI reflection - a gentle emotional record over the week, never a grade. Voice recognition runs in the browser only; pictures and reflections are stored in the Singapore-hosted, PDPA-compliant workspace with row-level security.
