Built on our own phonics IP, paired with licensed readers

Phonics Studio runs on Busy Bees’ in-house synthetic-phonics scope and sequence - the same sound progression our educators teach in centre - so home practice never contradicts the classroom. The decodable titles shown here are drawn from a licensed reader library mapped to that sequence, and the read-along voice is produced inside our Singapore Azure Neural TTS tenant. Teachers stay in control: they set the focus sounds, the level and the assignments, and nothing reaches a child that a teacher has not chosen.
