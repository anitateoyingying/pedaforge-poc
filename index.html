The Shared Challenge

One Sector-Wide Crisis, Four Fronts

Across our pilot centres, educators lose hours to documentation, leaders lack the data to coach, and physical learning environments are planned on paper — and the home learning environment is invisible to the centre. Each proposal attacks one front of the same problem.

~40%

Documentation Overload

A 2025 NIE working paper found Singaporean ECE educators spend up to 40% of working hours on manual portfolio documentation, forcing a "pedagogical compromise" away from individualised, child-led interaction.

Siloed

Leadership Without Data

Centre Directors lack the bandwidth to audit lessons or provide daily micro-coaching, and annual appraisals stay subjective without continuous observation data, leaving teachers unsupported (TODAY, Feb 2026).

Manual

Environments & Resources

Classroom layouts are drafted on paper and inventories live on isolated spreadsheets, so spaces are cluttered, resources duplicated or lost, and HQ has no visibility for safe, evidence-based budgeting.

Invisible

The Home Literacy Gap

Parents want to support early reading but lack structured phonics and shared-reading guidance at home, and centres have zero visibility into home practice — so learning stalls the moment the child leaves the gate.

Shared Posture

Enterprise-Grade, Singapore-Resident, PDPA-Compliant

All four proposals are delivered by the same operator on a common technology foundation and data-protection posture: a React front end and FastAPI back end, hosted on Azure in the Singapore region. Classroom and Leadership are AI-token-intensive (Azure OpenAI closed tenant); SproutSpace is the broadest custom build, standing on permissive open-source libraries for its layout canvas, QR tracking and dashboards; and PedaForge Home adds Azure AI Speech and Neural TTS for its reading coach and talking dictionary - all woven through the same enclave.

Data Residency & PDPA

All child, educator, and centre data is hosted in Singapore-based data centres and handled in strict accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

SG Region PDPA

Zero Public AI Training & RBAC

PedaForge uses Azure OpenAI within a closed tenant - no child or staff data trains public models. Role-based access control ensures each user sees only their authorised cohorts.

Closed Tenant RBAC

Aligned to National Frameworks

Every module maps to Singapore's ECE frameworks - EYDF (0–3), NEL/iTeach (4–6), the ECDA Quality Teaching Tool (QTT), and the Skills Framework for ECCE.

EYDF NEL QTT SFw

Independently Fundable

Each proposal has its own itemised budget and KPIs with no shared-cost double-counting. ECDA can approve any combination without budget overlap or hidden dependency.
