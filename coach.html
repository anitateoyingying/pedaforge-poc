AI Coach: Reflective Practice Mode

Active QTT-Aligned
PF
Welcome! I'm your PedaForge Coach. Let's reflect on today's teaching. Can you describe a moment from today where you felt a child was particularly engaged, or particularly struggling?
You
During our 'Community Helpers' activity, Leo got frustrated when building the fire station. He threw the blocks and started crying.
PF
That sounds like a significant moment. Before we explore strategies, tell me: what was your immediate reaction when Leo threw the blocks?
QTT: Teacher-Child Interaction
You
I sat at his eye level, acknowledged his anger, and offered him a different tool.
PF
Excellent emotional scaffolding. You instinctively acted as Leo's Key Person by getting to his level and validating his feelings. For tomorrow, consider guiding Leo to articulate what frustrated him before offering alternatives. This builds his self-regulation vocabulary. What specific words might you use?
