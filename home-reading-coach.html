AI Reading Coach

A playful read-at-home buddy that listens to 5 - 6 year olds read decodable passages aloud, gives gentle word-by-word encouragement, and quietly powers a teacher-confirmed view of accuracy, fluency and prosody. The child sees a friendly garden; the teacher sees the rigour - and confirms every recording before it counts.

Why two views
The same recording serves a 5 - 6 year old as a friendly garden and a teacher as a QTT-aligned assessment. The child stays an active, motivated learner - monitoring their own reading without a score - while the educator keeps full professional judgement over what is recorded.
Under the Hood

Playful for the Child, Rigorous for the Teacher

One recording, two carefully separated experiences. Every AI signal is advisory and teacher-confirmed before it touches a child’s record.

Listens with Real Browser STT

This demo listens with the browser’s real speech recognition and aligns each word against the passage - here catching the missing final /d/ in “pond” and the ‘sh’ digraph in “ship”. In production, Azure AI Speech pronunciation assessment scores accuracy, fluency and prosody at the word and phoneme level.

Browser Speech RecognitionAzure AI Speech in production

Coaches with Neural TTS

When a word needs another try, real browser TTS replays it in a clear, friendly voice so the child can listen and echo (Azure Neural TTS in production). Feedback stays gentle and growth-focused - never a red cross, never a score in front of the child.

Teacher Holds the Pen

Because child voices and accents vary, every score is advisory. Nothing reaches Amira’s record until the teacher reviews the clip and confirms - keeping professional judgement, not the model, in charge of the verdict.
