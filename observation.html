How It Works

1 Capture - type an anecdotal note the moment you see it; AI tags it to an ECDA QTT indicator with a met / emerging judgement and rationale.
2 Synthesise - once you have two or more notes, AI drafts a balanced report: strengths, growth areas, and a follow-up plan.
3 Decide - you edit every section before saving. The AI prepares, the leader decides.

Saved observations are visible to you; centre directors can review all observations across the team.

Close the Loop

Every saved observation feeds the centre-wide professional development picture and the educator's appraisal record.
