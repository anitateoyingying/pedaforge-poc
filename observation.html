During the lesson

Actual Observation

Mdm Lim captures multimodal notes - quick voice memos and typed lines - on a centre-managed HQ device. As each note lands, PedaForge transcribes it on-device and auto-tags the anecdotal evidence to the targeted QTT indicators in real time, so the Director stays present with the children instead of buried in a clipboard.

After the lesson

Post-Observation & Follow-Up

PedaForge synthesises today’s evidence against Sarah’s historical QTT trajectory, drafts a balanced feedback summary, and proposes an updated follow-up plan. Mdm Lim reviews and edits before the appraisal conversation - the AI prepares, the leader decides.

Draft Feedback Summary AI-synthesised · editable
Sarah showed clear, sustained growth in Teacher-Child Interaction: her response to Leo - getting to eye level and naming the emotion before offering a solution - is exactly the emotional scaffolding flagged in her last IDP review, now consistently evidenced. Her water-table exchange with Amira marks a genuine step forward on her Sustained Shared Thinking goal: she posed a true open question and extended the child’s thinking rather than closing it. The one growth point is transition management - the pack-away instruction was given once to the whole group and a few children disengaged. This is a small environment-and-routine adjustment, not an interaction concern, and a natural next focus.

Follow-Up Plan

  • 1 Mark the SST goal as met. Update Sarah’s IDP to evidence sustained shared thinking with today’s anecdotal record attached, and set a stretch goal: model SST during a small-group inquiry next term.
  • 2 Target transitions. Co-plan one visual pack-away routine with Sarah and revisit it in a 10-minute walkthrough in two weeks - pair her with Siti R., whose K2 transitions are a centre strength.
    SFw for ECCE · Manage the Learning Environment (ECE-LEC-3001-1.1, WSQ)
  • 3 Feed into the appraisal. Carry this summary into the half-yearly appraisal conversation and into the Leadership Dashboard so the centre PD plan reflects Sarah’s progress.
