Talking Dictionary

A child-safe word lab where a young reader types or taps a word, hears it spoken aloud with natural Azure Neural text-to-speech, then sees the syllables, a kid-friendly meaning and an example sentence. Sentence starters invite the child to use the word, a Spell it! game turns recognition into production, and a My Words Jar makes growth visible - all on their own, at their own pace.

How this helps a young reader grow

Every word a child looks up becomes a small loop of independent learning: hear it, see how it breaks into sounds, understand what it means, use it in a sentence of their own, then spell it back. Because the Talking Dictionary speaks with natural Azure Neural voices and never marks a child wrong, it builds the confidence to keep trying. Words a child practises move from New to Learning to Known in their Words Jar, so progress is visible and motivating - to the child, the parent and the teacher.

Child-safe by design

  • Only age-appropriate words and definitions from a curated early-reading dictionary - no open web search.
  • Encouraging feedback only - the child is never told they are wrong, just nudged to try again.
  • Natural Azure Neural voices read every word, so the child can learn pronunciation independently.
  • Singapore-hosted, PDPA-compliant - the Words Jar belongs to the child and family.
