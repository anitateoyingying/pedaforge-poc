Proposal Overview

Reading is Learned at School, but Loved at Home

PedaForge Home is the family-facing bridge in the Agape's Sandbox programme. It carries the same systematic, operator-owned literacy pedagogy that Busy Bees teachers use in the classroom directly into the living room - as songs, stories, games and gentle daily practice that parents can run with confidence, even when English is not the family's first language.

It deliberately serves two audiences at once: the family at home, who gain simple tools and clear next steps, and the teacher at the centre, who sequences the learning, reads a shared termly benchmark and confirms every AI judgement before it counts.

English is the medium of instruction from Primary 1. By building a strong, joyful home reading habit at K1 and K2, we close the gap before it compounds - turning the home into a deliberate partner in early literacy, not a variable left to chance.

S$140,000 over 12 months

ECDA Innovation Sandbox. A self-contained submission with its own budget, KPIs and 12-month plan, deployable across our pilot families and centres.

Serves the Family

PedaForge Home completes the programme by addressing the one place the other three proposals cannot reach. PedaForge Classroom serves the child, PedaForge Leadership serves the educator, and SproutSpace serves the environment - PedaForge Home serves the family, building the home-school bridge that makes every other gain stick.

The Problem

English Standards Begin at Home

The home is the strongest lever in early literacy - and the one we currently leave to chance. Singapore's own evidence is unambiguous: what happens around the bookshelf at home shapes how a child reads at school.

2.1

The Home is the Strongest Predictor

Singapore research shows the home literacy environment - especially how often families share books and how interested the child is in reading - is among the strongest predictors of early oral language and word reading. Sun & Ng's large local study of around 1,440 children validated a four-factor model of the Singapore home literacy environment, confirming that home practice, not just school instruction, drives early outcomes.

2.2

Very Uneven Home English Exposure

Singapore's language shift means English exposure at home varies enormously from family to family. Because English is the medium of instruction from Primary 1, children who arrive at school with a weak home foundation start behind - and that gap compounds across the critical K2-to-P1 transition rather than closing on its own.

2.3

No Shared Bridge, No Parent Confidence

Today there is no shared home-school reading benchmark - parents and teachers cannot see the same picture of a child's progress. Many parents lack the tools or confidence to read with their child the way the research recommends. Dialogic, conversation-rich shared reading is powerful, but it is rarely modelled for families, so the home effort stays well-meaning but unguided.

Local research grounding
The case for a home-school bridge rests on Singapore's own evidence base: Sun & Ng's study of approximately 1,440 children, which validated a four-factor model of the Singapore home literacy environment and linked it to early language and word-reading outcomes. PedaForge Home operationalises that evidence as a practical, daily tool - giving every family the systematic phonics, decodable readers and dialogic strategies that the research shows matter most, and giving teachers a shared termly benchmark to read the same picture parents see.
The Solution

One Bridge, Five Connected Modules

PedaForge Home pairs operator-owned phonics and decodable readers with a shared termly benchmark, an advisory AI reading coach, an SEL writing portal and a child-safe talking dictionary - so every family has a clear, joyful path from first sounds to confident reading.

1

Phonics Studio & Decodable Reader Library

Our operator-owned systematic phonics programme, delivered into the home as songs, stories and games children genuinely enjoy. Alongside it sits a curated library of levelled and decodable readers that teachers sequence per child - so the books a family opens at home always match the sounds the child is currently learning.

Termly Benchmarking & Home-School Reading Bridge

Success Metrics

Measurable, Evidence-Backed KPIs

Every target is tied to a concrete data source generated inside the platform - in-app reading logs, termly benchmark records and a pictorial disposition survey - so impact can be verified, not asserted.

Weekly Shared Reading: 25% → 70%

The share of pilot families reading together at least weekly rises from roughly 25% at baseline to 70% by Month 12, building a durable home reading habit.

Source: in-app log & parent confirmation

75% Advance ≥1 Benchmark Band

Across two consecutive terms, 75% of children progress at least one band - Emerging, Developing or Secure - on the shared termly reading benchmark, from a 0% baseline.

Source: termly benchmark records

Love of Reading: +0.6 on a 5-Point Scale

Children's love-of-reading disposition rises by at least 0.6 on a 5-point pictorial survey, with 80% or more of children landing at the “enjoy” level or higher by the end of the pilot.

Source: pre/post pictorial survey
Alignment & Compliance

Anchored in National Frameworks, Built for Trust

PedaForge Home is designed to sit squarely inside Singapore's early childhood landscape - aligned to national curriculum and SEL frameworks, complementary to existing national reading programmes, and secured to the same standard as the rest of the Sandbox.

Curriculum & Sector Alignment

  • Aligned to the NEL Framework (2022) Language & Literacy learning area and its three Cs - Communication, Conversation and Connection - plus the iTeach principles.
  • Builds social-emotional learning (SEL) through the Draw, Write & Reflect portal, supporting disposition and growth mindset alongside literacy.
  • Complements, rather than duplicates, national reading programmes - NLB's Early READ, kidsREAD@Home and Let's Read - by adding systematic phonics and a shared benchmark.
  • Builds staff capability through the Skills Framework for Early Childhood Care & Education (SFw for ECCE) and WSQ-aligned training.

Secure & Compliant by Design

  • Application, database and AI inference run in the Azure Singapore region for full data residency.
  • Strict PDPA compliance for all family, child and reading data, with explicit consent flows for every family.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) separates what parents, teachers and leaders can see and do.
  • Zero public AI training - no child or family data is ever used to train public models; AI runs in a closed Azure tenant.
Technology

Buy the Plumbing, Build the Pedagogy

PedaForge Home runs on the same React + FastAPI + PostgreSQL + Azure OpenAI stack as the other three proposals. We buy the undifferentiated infrastructure and spend the engineering budget where the value is - on the proprietary literacy IP only Busy Bees can create.

Build the IP, License the Commodity

We license what is commodity: Azure AI Speech for pronunciation and oral-reading scoring (always advisory and teacher-confirmed), Azure Neural TTS for natural audio, a third-party levelled-reader subscription, and a permissive open-source canvas library for the drawing portal. We build in-house the fundable IP: the systematic phonics engine, our bespoke decodable readers, the termly benchmark model, the SEL reflection model, the child-safe talking-dictionary state machine, and the consent / PDPA layer. AI value is highest in Module 3 (Reading Coach) and Module 5 (Talking Dictionary), where AI is roughly 50% or more of the experience.

React + FastAPI + PostgreSQL Azure OpenAI & AI Speech Bespoke Decodables Permissive OSS Libraries

Where the Engineering Goes

  • Build: phonics engine, bespoke decodable readers (the fundable IP), termly benchmark model, SEL reflection model and the kid-dictionary state machine.
  • License: Azure AI Speech for oral-reading scoring, Azure Neural TTS for audio, a third-party reader subscription and an OSS canvas library.
  • Guardrail: every AI judgement in the Reading Coach is advisory and confirmed by a teacher before it is recorded against a child.
  • Concentrate AI: AI is roughly 50% or more of the experience in Modules 3 and 5, and a lighter assist elsewhere.
