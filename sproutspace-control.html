Why HQ Visibility Matters

One Network View, Safer and Smarter Budgets

Before SproutSpace, HQ had no live picture of what its centres owned, how it was used, or whether new layouts were safe. Spreadsheets aged the moment they were saved, resources were duplicated or quietly lost, and budgets were set on guesswork.

The leadership payoff

This control view turns the whole network into evidence. Real usage rates and durability data size the next order, the 32% cut in duplicate purchasing across centres flows straight from shared visibility, and centralised standards mean a single safety or brand update reaches every room at once. Layout approvals close the loop - supporting the sandbox KPI of 100% first-time classroom-layout safety approval - so HQ governs by data, not by site visits and paperwork.
