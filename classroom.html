PedaForge Classroom

Pedagogy & Differentiated Learning Sandbox. A 12-month ECDA Innovation Sandbox proposal from Busy Bees, piloted across our centres, that returns documentation hours to educators and turns every lesson into a differentiated, evidence-led experience for the Unique Child.

Overview Lesson Planner Portfolio Work Sample
The Problem

The Pedagogical Compromise

Our educators want to teach responsively, but the administrative load makes it impossible. The cost is paid in lost child-led interaction and a slide toward one-size-fits-all planning.

~40%

Documentation Overload

A 2025 NIE working paper found Singaporean early childhood educators spend up to ~40% of their working hours on manual portfolio documentation. Hours that should belong to children are consumed by paperwork.

Burnout

Unsustainable Demands

The Straits Times (Apr 2025) reported unsustainable parental documentation demands driving educators toward emotional exhaustion. The result is less individualised, child-led interaction and a default to one-size-fits-all planning.

The compromise, in one line

When an educator must choose between documenting a moment and being present for it, the documentation usually wins, and the child loses the responsive interaction that quality early childhood practice depends on. PedaForge Classroom removes that trade-off.

The Solution

Two Modules, One Continuous Loop

A profile-driven planner and an authentic portfolio engine that feed each other: observations sharpen profiles, and richer profiles produce better-differentiated lessons. Every output maps to EYDF, NEL, and Busy Bees curriculum standards.

1

Profile-Driven Smart Lesson Planning

The planner ingests aggregated class profiles, learning needs, learning styles, and abilities, then generates differentiated scaffolding and automatically schedules Rehearse & Retrieve prompts to sustain an Enabling Environment. Every plan is mapped to EYDF, NEL, and Busy Bees curriculum standards.

Worked example · Theme: Community Helpers

Class profile: 3 visual-spatial learners, 1 sensory-avoidant child, 2 advanced-verbal learners. The planner proposes a Fire Station dramatic-play centre with blueprints for the visual-spatial group, noise-dampening foam blocks for the sensory-avoidant child, and a ‘Dispatcher’ narrator role to stretch the advanced-verbal learners.

EYDF NEL Busy Bees

2

Authentic Portfolio & Dynamic Profiling Engine

Educators capture daily observations, work samples, and anecdotal records on mobile in seconds. The AI drafts developmental narratives tagged to EYDF and NEL milestones, building each child’s Living Profile and compiling term Summary Reports. A feedback loop pushes profile insights straight back into the planner, and generates Targeted Suggestions for Parents.

Measurable Outcomes

Key Performance Indicators

Three outcome targets, each tied to an independent, auditable data source so ECDA can verify impact without relying on self-reporting alone.

Documentation Time: ~8h → under 3h

Average documentation effort drops from ~8 hours to under 3 hours per educator per week, with zero off-hours administration.

Data: time diaries + platform analytics

100% Differentiated Lessons

100% of AI-generated lessons include explicit scaffolding for 2 or more distinct learner profiles, eliminating one-size-fits-all planning.

Data: lesson-plan metadata audit

100% Accurate Tagging in 60s

100% of uploaded work samples are accurately tagged to the correct EYDF / NEL milestones within 60 seconds of upload.

Data: tagging logs
Architecture & Compliance

Enterprise-Grade, Singapore-Resident, PDPA-Compliant

Built on a React front end and FastAPI back end with a dedicated Azure AI Enclave (Azure OpenAI in a closed tenant, Singapore region), so child and educator data never leaves a controlled, auditable environment.

React + FastAPI + Azure AI Enclave

A React single-page front end and a FastAPI (Python) back end, paired with Azure OpenAI inside a closed tenant hosted in the Singapore region, keeping inference and storage within national borders.

React FastAPI Azure OpenAI SG Region

PDPA & Role-Based Access

All data is handled in strict accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures each educator and director sees only their authorised cohorts.

PDPA RBAC

Zero Public AI Training

No child or staff data is ever used to train public models. The closed tenant guarantees that all data stays within Busy Bees’ controlled enclave.

Closed Tenant No Public Training
Equipment

Hardware in Every Classroom

Capture-ready devices placed directly in learning spaces so observation happens in the moment, not in front of a desktop after hours.

55 Tablets, Rugged Cases & Stylus

Five complete capture sets per centre, totalling 55 tablets, each with a rugged protective case and stylus for fast, durable, in-classroom documentation of observations and work samples.

55 tablets 5 sets per centre Rugged case + stylus

Why in-classroom devices

Mobile capture at the point of learning is what makes under-3-hour documentation achievable: educators record observations as they happen, and the AI drafts the narrative, instead of reconstructing the day later from memory.
